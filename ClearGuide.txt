Routewise is a web-based platform designed to help companies track vehicles, operational data, and relevant business information from a browser. The project is intended as a practical learning initiative with the potential to become a monetized product over time.

The core idea is to provide businesses with a simple way to monitor trucks, fleet activity, and operational details through a local-hosted system unless otherwise stated. Companies will connect to the platform in a controlled environment, and the project will prioritize usability, affordability, and clarity.

## Project Vision

Routewise aims to give companies a modern, minimal, and easy-to-use interface for managing operational visibility. The platform should be straightforward for day-to-day use and accessible to non-technical users.

## Core Product Goals

- Web-based access from a browser
- Modern, minimal design
- Simple and intuitive navigation
- Company admin panels for managing users and settings
- Ability to add and remove users from the admin panel
- Open-source foundation for future development
- Low-cost approach to keep the product accessible
- New companies receive 10 free credits before needing paid access
- Clear communication and support channels

## Business and Operational Direction

The project is intended to balance usefulness with sustainability. It should remain cost-conscious while delivering enough value to justify adoption. The system should be practical for businesses that need visibility into vehicle operations and related information without unnecessary complexity.

## Support Model

A strong support structure is part of the product vision. The project aims to provide:

- AI-powered support available 24/7
- Live support during the day for more direct assistance
- Clear lines of communication for enquiries and follow-up
- Public documentation and updates through the project YouTube channel
